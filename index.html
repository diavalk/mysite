Hello, I am

Hi there!

My name is Diana Kozlovska. I am a researcher and computer science student specializing in cybersecurity, telecommunications, and autonomous systems. I am currently studying at National University "Kyiv Aviation Institute" and Universidad de Cádiz as an Erasmus+ scholar, with an upcoming academic exchange at the University of Gothenburg in spring of 2027.

My primary research centers on cyber-resilience for autonomous robotics and drones, post-quantum cryptographic transitions, quantum key distribution channel authentication, and smart radio system signal characteristics.
